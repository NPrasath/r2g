# Lab 01 · Collect and approve requirements

**Lead:** BA. **Partner:** Tester. Developer/TM contribute constraints.

1. Read [REQ-RQC-001](../../docs/requirements/REQ-RQC-001.md). Capture the operator problem and open decisions in Excel.
2. Select Requirements Analyst and use the BA prompt in [the Copilot guide](../../docs/copilot-guide.md). Review its questions and assumptions.
3. Interview the assigned process owner. Resolve D01–D08 or explicitly defer items with a narrower agreed first-task scope.
4. Ask the Tester to challenge the criteria with partial/final batches, mixed outcomes, holds, repeats, errors, permissions, and concurrency.
5. Create a requirement issue using the form; record the human business decision, date, version, acceptance IDs, and linked document. Keep unresolved material rules blocking implementation.
6. Link the Excel requirement row to the issue and submit the documentation change through a PR.

**Deliverables:** interview/decision record, GitHub requirement, revised document, QA testability assessment, business approval where actually given.

**Exit:** the first task's business behavior has an explicit owner-approved specification. A polished AI draft with unanswered material questions is not approved.
